How It Works

Follow these simple steps to assess your website's vulnerabilities:

  1. Step 1: Enter Your Website URL

    Simply enter the URL of your website into our scan tool. CyberCheck will begin the scanning process immediately.

  2. Step 2: Scan for Vulnerabilities

    Our advanced algorithms will check for potential security flaws like SQL Injection, Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), and Open Port vulnerabilities.

  3. Step 3: View Your Scan Results

    Once the scan is complete, you'll receive a detailed report highlighting the vulnerabilities detected and suggested fixes.

  4. Step 4: Implement Fixes

    Based on the recommendations in the report, implement the necessary fixes to secure your website from potential threats.

It’s that easy! Start using CyberCheck now to secure your site.
